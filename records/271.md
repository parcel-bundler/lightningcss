## Draft reply
Status update: this still doesn't happen on the latest release. For adjacent rules it can be done safely, but only when no shared declaration overlaps another declaration in either rule. That covers the same property, a shorthand and its longhands, logical and physical pairs like `width`/`inline-size`, and `all`. The selectors also have to be safe to combine into one list. As noted above, it only helps when the shared declarations are longer than the extra selectors.

On real stylesheets the raw saving is about 0.6–1.3% for Bootstrap, Bulma and Radix Themes, and close to zero for others. Much of it comes from repeated custom properties, and there's an open draft PR, #1333, that factors those out. After gzip/brotli the gain is a lot smaller.
