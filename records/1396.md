# #1396 `@media not (width = 10px)` is printed as `@media (width = 10px)`

MediaCondition::to_css prints `not (range feature)` by inverting the comparison operator; MediaFeatureComparison::negate maps `=` to `=`, so the `not` is dropped and the query is inverted. Affects `not (width = 10px)`, `not (10px = width)`, `screen and (not (width = 10px))`, nested `(not (width = 10px)) and ...`, any feature (resolution, color), and the lowered form for old targets (`not (width = 10px)` -> `(width:10px)` for Safari 12). `@media not screen and (...)` (query qualifier) and `@container` are unaffected.

## Draft reply

Thanks, confirmed. Since 1.29.0, a negated range feature is printed by flipping its operator, and `=` has no opposite, so `@media not (width = 10px)` comes out as `@media (width=10px)` and the rule applies under the opposite condition. 1.28.2 kept the `not`. It affects any feature (`resolution`, `color`), the reversed `not (10px = width)` form, nested conditions, and the lowered output for older targets (`(width:10px)`). `@media not screen and (...)` and `@container` aren't affected.

The fix is to keep the `not` for equality instead of inverting it. Until then, writing the condition without `not`, e.g. `(width < 10px), (width > 10px)`, avoids it.
