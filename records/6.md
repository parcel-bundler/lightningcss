## Draft reply

Closing this tracking issue, since every remaining item is either decided or tracked separately:

- Local-by-default scoping, local animation/counter names, `:local()`/`:global()` and `composes` are supported.
- The non-function `:local`/`:global` form isn't supported, because its scope applies to everything to its right, which makes it ambiguous. Use `:global(.foo)` instead.
- `:export` is tracked in #404.
- `@value` is tracked in #659. For sharing values between files, CSS variables with `dashedIdents` and `var(--name from "./file.css")` are the supported alternative.

The full list is in the docs: https://lightningcss.dev/css-modules.html#unsupported-features
