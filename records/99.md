## Draft reply (status comment)

Status update on Color 5 as of 1.33.0:

**Supported:** `color-mix()` (explicit color space, two colors), relative color syntax, `light-dark()` (colors, and now images), and `alpha()` (new in 1.33.0).

**Missing:**
- `contrast-color()`: now shipped in Chrome, Firefox and Safari. There are two open PRs, #808 and #1160, that address it.
- The newer `color-mix()` forms (optional color space, more than two colors): tracked in #1224.
- The `display-p3-linear` color space: tracked in #1161.
- `device-cmyk()`, `@color-profile` and custom color spaces: no browser supports them and the spec marks them at risk. They pass through unchanged.

Unsupported syntax currently passes through as written, without being lowered for older browsers.
