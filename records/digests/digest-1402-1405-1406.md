## Shared root cause?
Partly. All three are about calc results, but the fixes are separate:
- #1402 (type check, `Calc::add`/`parse_sum`) and #1402 (sign fold) are both in src/values/calc.rs. One PR could cover both.
- #1405 is in number/dimension serialization (no NaN handling), plus the missing top-level censoring. It pairs naturally with #1397 (top-level clamp to the property range) as one "finalize top-level calc" step.
- #1406 is in src/values/resolution.rs (no Calc impl), independent.

Related: #1404 (errorRecovery keeps grammar-invalid declarations as unparsed, with no warning). Fixing #1402's type check would make `calc(1 + 1px)` follow the #1404 path instead of being typed as valid.
Side observation: a unitless top-level length (`width:10`) is "repaired" to `10px` (length.rs, `// TODO: quirks mode only?`). It's not reported in this batch.
