# #1235 Stale StyleRuleKey hash (missed dedup, lost rules)

**bug, confirmed, critical, queue-fix (PR #1236 needs the redesign the maintainer asked for)**

Still present on master. `StyleRuleKey` caches its hash, but merges change the rule in place and cascade merges pop rules without removing their keys. The result: a deterministic missed dedup, plus a per-process chance (ahash random seed) of deleting a live rule.

Master state (`1235-repro.mjs`, `--repeat 20`, plus a 50-process loop):
- 300 distinct copies of the comment's pattern, each in its **own** parent: master lost at least one `[aria-hidden]` rule in **48/50** processes. 1.33.0 and 1.30.2 behave the same (15-19 distinct outputs in 20 runs).
- The same 300 copies in **one** rule list lose a rule in only 1-2/50. Amplifying needs many small lists. That matters for the "test that fails reliably" the maintainer asked for on #1236.
- Missed dedup from the issue body: wrong on every version, every run.
- Control (plain duplicate rule): correct.

Real-world impact: Tailwind v4 (`optimize()` → minify), rspack, Canva and CI flakiness, with 9 +1s.

PR #1236: it fixes the root cause and has good tests. On 2026-11-21 the maintainer asked for rehash-on-lookup with FxHash instead, because the PR is quadratic with CSS modules. We're waiting on the author. No new reply is needed on the issue.
